# Sierra Crest Passes — The Crossing Log

Dave Dell'Aquila's quest to drive every paved pass on the Sierra crest:
29 passes, tracked on a map (topographic / satellite / 3D terrain) with an
elevation-ranked list, a detail card per pass, and an elevation profile.

**Live page:** https://davedellaquila.github.io/sierra-crest-passes/

- `index.html` is the whole page — self-contained except for the map
  libraries and tile layers it loads from their CDNs.
- Crossed passes are baked into the page (13 of 29 as of Nov 16, 2026).
  When Dave crosses another pass, the crossed set in `index.html` is
  updated and this repo is pushed again.
- Live road cameras (added Nov 25, 2026): passes with a Caltrans CCTV camera at or near the summit show its still in the detail card — credited to Caltrans, captioned with what the camera actually shows, auto-refreshed every two minutes, tap to enlarge; a few passes carry linked resort camera pages instead (Kirkwood for Carson, Sierra-at-Tahoe for Echo, Bear Valley for Ebbetts/Pacific Grade). Sonora Pass shows a live still from Dodge Ridge’s Summit Cam, and Ebbetts Pass + Pacific Grade Summit show Bear Valley’s Summit Cam (both are YouTube live streams; the page embeds each stream’s live thumbnail still, refreshed in place and credited to the resort). Passes with no camera near them show none.
