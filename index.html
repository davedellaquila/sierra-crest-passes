How the crest reads

The rust line joins the passes in latitude order, north to south, so you can trace the spine at a glance. It is a crest diagram, not a driving route — several passes sit on spurs (US-395 summits, Nevada-side crossings) just off the main divide.

Season note

The high passes — Tioga, Sonora, Ebbetts, Monitor, Kaiser — are seasonal and close with snow. Carson (SR-88) and Donner Summit (I-80) are the all-weather workhorses. Plan the remaining high ones for late spring through fall.
